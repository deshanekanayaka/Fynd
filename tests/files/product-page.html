See where your energy goes

Watt Watcher reads your smart meter every half hour and shows which appliances cost you the most. A household on our plan cuts its yearly bill by about 90 pounds, because the app names the three habits that cost most.

What it does

The app reads your half hourly meter data, compares this week with the same week last year, and warns you when a bill is about to rise. It needs no extra hardware, because your smart meter already sends the readings.

What it does not do

Watt Watcher forecasts nothing. It shows you what already happened, and a household that wants next month's bill must work it out alone.
